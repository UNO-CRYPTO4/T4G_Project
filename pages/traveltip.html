Travel Tips

Pack light, pack smart

Roll clothes instead of folding to save space, and always carry one change of clothes in your hand luggage.

Check entry requirements early

Visa rules and passport validity requirements vary by destination — confirm these at least a month before departure.

Keep copies of important documents

Store digital and physical copies of your passport, itinerary, and travel insurance separately from the originals.

Learn a few local phrases

Simple greetings and "thank you" in the local language go a long way toward a warmer travel experience.
