Travel Smarter. Travel Better.

Simple travel tips to help you prepare, explore confidently and make the most of every Uno Tours experience.

YOUR JOURNEY STARTS HERE

A little preparation goes a long way.

Whether you're traveling for the first time or you're an experienced explorer, being prepared can make your journey smoother, safer and more enjoyable.

01

Travel Documents

Keep your passport, visa, identification and booking documents safe and easily accessible throughout your trip.

  • Check passport validity.
  • Confirm visa requirements.
  • Keep digital copies of important documents.
02

Pack Smart

Pack according to your destination, activities and expected weather. Avoid carrying things you won't need.

  • Check the weather before packing.
  • Carry versatile clothing.
  • Keep essentials in your hand luggage.
03

Money & Payments

Plan how you'll access money while traveling and familiarize yourself with the local currency.

  • Carry a small amount of local currency.
  • Notify your bank before international travel.
  • Keep payment cards secure.
04

Stay Connected

Make sure you can communicate and access important information while you're away.

  • Check international roaming options.
  • Consider a local SIM or eSIM.
  • Save important contact numbers offline.
05

Respect Local Culture

Every destination has its own traditions, customs and ways of life. Learn about them before you arrive.

  • Learn basic local customs.
  • Dress appropriately for cultural sites.
  • Ask before photographing people.
06

Stay Safe

Stay aware of your surroundings and take sensible precautions wherever your journey takes you.

  • Keep valuables secure.
  • Stay aware of your surroundings.
  • Follow local safety guidance.
BEFORE YOU GO

Your pre-travel checklist.

Take a few minutes before departure to make sure the essentials are covered.

Confirm your booking details.
Check your passport and travel documents.
Check the weather at your destination.
Prepare medications and personal essentials.
Inform someone you trust about your itinerary.
Save important contacts and booking information.
