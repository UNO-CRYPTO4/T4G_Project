01

Introduction

At Uno Tours, we respect your privacy and aim to handle personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you use our website, contact us, create an account or make a travel enquiry or booking.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with Uno Tours, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Email address and telephone number.
  • Account information when you register.
  • Travel preferences and booking details.
  • Information you provide when contacting our support team.
03

How We Use Information

Information may be used to help us provide and manage our services.

  • Process and manage enquiries.
  • Assist with travel bookings.
  • Respond to customer questions.
  • Maintain and improve our website.
  • Communicate important information about your booking or service.
04

Booking Information

When you make a booking or enquiry, certain information may be required to arrange the requested travel service.

Customers should ensure that the information they provide is accurate and up to date.

Only provide information that is necessary for your enquiry, account or travel arrangements.

05

Information Sharing

Uno Tours does not treat customer information as public information.

Where necessary to provide a requested travel service, relevant information may need to be shared with appropriate service providers or partners involved in fulfilling the booking.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or legitimate legal obligations.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through our website and services.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality and improve the user experience.

Cookies are small files stored on a device when visiting certain websites. Depending on your browser, you may be able to control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

08

Your Choices

You may contact Uno Tours if you have questions about information you have provided to us.

Depending on the nature of your request and applicable requirements, you may ask us about accessing, correcting or updating information associated with your account or enquiry.

09

Children's Privacy

Our website and travel services are not specifically directed toward children.

We encourage parents or guardians to supervise the online activities of children in their care.

10

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
